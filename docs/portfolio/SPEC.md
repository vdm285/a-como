# Showpiece portfolio — shared spec

Three rough but working demos of how A cómo could open and how its timeline could feel, for the owner to pick from (his playbook: big UI and motion decisions are a portfolio of three working demos, not prose). Each is one HTML file in `docs/portfolio/`, published as an artifact after review. The losing options stay here for reference.

## What every demo must do

- **Real data only.** Everything comes from `window.DEMO` in `docs/portfolio/demo-data.js` (generated by `tools/portfolio-data.mjs` from the pipeline). Never type a number, a store name or a date into the page; compute it from DEMO. Any sentence that states a fact must be derivable from DEMO, and the computation must be the honest one already provided in `DEMO.stories` (matched stores, recent window). No invented weeks, no smoothing presented as data. Motion between two real weekly states (a tween) is fine as motion as long as the label shows the real week and the tween ends exactly on the real values.
- **Credit visible** on every screen: `DEMO.credit`, small, bottom.
- **Spanish (Mexico)** for every word on screen.
- **The look of A cómo**, one deliberate dark world: background `#0c1118`, ink `#f4f5f7`, secondary ink `#b9bec7`, muted `#8a919c`, cheaper `#3987e5`, dearer `#e66767`, median grey `#5b616a`, accent `#ece6d8` (a warm neutral; blue means "cheaper" and nothing else). Diverging colour = interpolate in OKLab or at least in RGB from cheaper through grey to dearer, capped at ±50 % unless the demo states another cap on screen. Body face: Manrope from Google Fonts (`https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Manrope:wght@400;600;800&display=swap`) with `system-ui, sans-serif` fallback. A display face is allowed for headlines if the direction calls for it (Google Fonts only).
- **Allowed network hosts, exactly**: `https://cdnjs.cloudflare.com` (scripts: d3 `https://cdnjs.cloudflare.com/ajax/libs/d3/7.9.0/d3.min.js`, three.js `https://cdnjs.cloudflare.com/ajax/libs/three.js/0.160.0/three.min.js`), `https://fonts.googleapis.com` and `https://fonts.gstatic.com` (fonts), and the local `demo-data.js` loaded with `<script src="demo-data.js"></script>` (it is inlined at publish time). Nothing else: no images from elsewhere, no fetch to other sites.
- **Artifact page contract**: no `<!doctype>`, `<html>`, `<head>` or `<body>` tags (the host wraps the page); a `<title>` and a `<style>` at the top; `:root { color-scheme: dark; }` with every colour as a token; `html, body { height: 100%; background: var(--bg); }`; works at 1600×900 and at 390×844 with no horizontal scroll and a 16 px side gutter for text; no `alert()`; respects `prefers-reduced-motion` (show the end state with a replay button instead of auto-playing).
- **Timing on the wall clock**: every animation timeline is driven by `performance.now()` from its start, never by summed per-frame deltas (a throttled frame rate must not slow the story down).
- **Ends usable**: after the opening, the reader can hover or tap stores (name, price, date), scrub the weeks, and replay. Under 300 KB of own code (the data file is ~300 KB on top).
- **No console errors.** Test with puppeteer-core and the installed Chrome (`C:/Program Files (x86)/Google/Chrome/Application/chrome.exe`) at `http://localhost:5175/docs/portfolio/<file>.html` (the Vite dev server serves the repo root). Take screenshots into `data/cache/portfolio/<name>/` and look at them.

## DEMO, in short

`DEMO.stores[i] = [name, chain, kind, lat, lon]` (kind ∈ super, mercado, central, tortilleria, tradicional, farmacia, conveniencia). `DEMO.geo` has GeoJSON `alcaldias`, `zmvm`, `roads`, `green`, `water` (WGS84). `DEMO.visits`: `weeks[88]`, `count[88]` (stores visited each week), `products[88]` (weekly prices recorded), `rows[i]` (a 0/1 string per store, one char per week), `total` with `totalWhat`. `DEMO.partialWeeks`: light weeks. `DEMO.jitomate` and `DEMO.tortilla`: `weeks[26]`, `ref` (recent city median), `n`, `prices[storeIndex] = [26 values or null]`, `latest[storeIndex] = [price, date]` (recent window only), `series` = 88 rows `[week, median, min, max, n]` of the city median per week. `DEMO.stories`: `tortillaKinds` (recent median and n by kind), `jitomate` (peak week, last full week, their medians, matched stores, median ratio of last/peak over matched stores), `index` (a week, the three cheapest and the dearest supermarkets by basket index, `indexByStore`). `DEMO.recent` is the recent window (4–31 Aug 2026).

## The three directions

### A. Respira — the survey breathing, then a price moving (2D)
The map is drawn on a canvas (or SVG) with d3-geo, framed on the stores. **Act 1 (about 7 s):** the 88 weeks pass at a steady wall-clock pace; each week, the stores PROFECO visited that week flash and fade like breath, light weeks visibly fainter; a strip of 88 bars builds along the bottom (stores visited per week) and a counter climbs to `DEMO.visits.total` with its `totalWhat` label. **Act 2:** the jitomate takes the stage: every store coloured against `ref`, the 26 weeks play (about 0.7 s each), a large number shows that week's city median (computed from `prices`), the curve draws as it goes, and the story lands: the jitomate's fall from its peak week over the matched stores (`DEMO.stories.jitomate`), stated with the number of stores and both weeks. **End:** the recent map with tags on the three cheapest stores, a scrubber over the 26 weeks, replay.

### B. Relieve — the city as a price landscape (3D, three.js)
The city's alcaldía outlines as faint lines on a dark plane; each store a column (instanced) standing where it is, height proportional to the jitomate price that week, colour by deviation from `ref`. The camera starts high and settles into a slow orbit at about 35–45° elevation. Play the 26 weeks: columns grow and shrink with an ease between real weeks (the label always shows the real week; the tween lands exactly on the real heights); a thin floor ring at `ref` height (a translucent plane) makes "above / below the median" readable. Hover or tap a column: store, price, date. A toggle switches to the tortilla, where tortillerías tower over supermarkets. A slider and play button. If WebGL is missing, say so and show the 2D map instead.

### C. Portada — the week's front page (editorial)
A front page laid over the full-bleed map: a large display headline, a deck line with the exact numbers and their source, and the map highlighting the stores the story is about. Three stories, paged with arrows, swipe and keys, auto-advancing every ~9 s: (1) **la tortilla** (`tortillaKinds`: the supermarket kilo against the tortillería kilo, with n each and the window), tortillerías and supermarkets lit in their two colours, a strip of every price; (2) **el jitomate** (`stories.jitomate`: the fall since the peak week over the matched stores), a sparkline of the 26 weekly medians, the map coloured for the last full week; (3) **la tienda más barata de la semana** (`stories.index`: the three cheapest supermarkets by basket index that week, their indices, the dearest for contrast; say plainly that the index compares each store with the city median on the products PROFECO saw there), the three stores ringed on the map. Each story names its week(s) and n. A button "Explorar el mapa" links to https://vdm285.github.io/compa-precio/.

## Review rubric (reviewers use this, fixers apply it)

1. Every number and name on screen traced to DEMO, with the right computation; no sentence claims more than the data says.
2. Credit visible; Spanish; the palette; blue only for cheaper.
3. Wall-clock timing (measure: throttle the CPU 4× with CDP and confirm the opening takes the same seconds).
4. 1600×900 and 390×844 both work; no horizontal scroll; text never clipped.
5. Console clean; only the allowed hosts requested (list the requests).
6. Ends usable: hover or tap, scrub, replay; reduced motion shows the end state.
7. The studio bar: would a world-class studio ship this as the opening of a product? Name the cheapest-looking part and fix it.
